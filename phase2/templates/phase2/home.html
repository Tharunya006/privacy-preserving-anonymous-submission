What happens when you report

  1. Write your report Describe what happened. Add a photo or a text file if you have one.

Built to protect people who speak up

Every report is handled the same careful way, from the moment you press submit.

Anonymous submission

No account. No login. No email. Your name and your internet address are never saved with your report.

Report protection

Your report is locked with strong encryption before it is saved. Only journalists approved by an administrator can open it.

Hidden photo details removed

Photos can secretly carry the place they were taken and the camera used. We remove those details automatically before anything is stored.

Status tracking

Use your private token to follow your report from Submitted to Under review to Closed. No sign-in needed.
