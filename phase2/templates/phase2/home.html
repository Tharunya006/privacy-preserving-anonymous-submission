Privacy Preserving Anonymous Submission System

A secure channel between anonymous sources and authorized journalists.

I am a source

No account. No login. No email. Anonymous sources are never registered in the user database and are never asked to identify themselves.

You receive a random token after submitting. It is the only way to check your report's status, so keep it safe.

Submit a report Check status

I am a journalist

Authorized receivers sign in to review submissions. Accounts are created by an administrator and must be explicitly authorized before first use.

Sign in

I am an administrator

Administrators create journalist accounts, authorize or disable them, and review the audit trail. There is no public administrator registration.

Admin sign in

What the system enforces

  • Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes.
  • Every protected route is checked on the server, not just hidden in the UI.
  • Journalists move through PENDING → AUTHORIZED → DISABLED under admin control.
  • Sessions are HttpOnly, SameSite, time-limited, and rebuilt at each sign-in.
  • Every state-changing form carries a CSRF token.
  • Submission tokens are random and stored only as a hash; no source IP is kept.
